Method

The same recipe for every model

1

Preprocessing

Sizes scaled (StandardScaler); colour and type one-hot; 16 material flags. Price as log. Name as TF-IDF or e5 vectors; photo as a frozen network’s vector (384–512 numbers).

2

Split

793 test listings (92 designs) locked away since the Midterm. Colour variants of one design always stay together (groups). Seed 42.

3

Tuning

Stratified grouped 5-fold CV. GridSearchCV or RandomizedSearchCV inside one sklearn Pipeline, so nothing leaks from the folds. Pick by CV mean, then one test run.

4

Checks

Per-class precision, recall and PR-AUC; design names masked; six fold seeds; outside tests on Amazon, IKEA Saudi Arabia and IKEA photos.

Libraries: scikit-learn, LightGBM, PyTorch / torchvision, Hugging Face transformers, sentence-transformers. The chosen models are saved with joblib and served by the app (Starlette API + React).

final.ipynb, sections 0, 1.3, 1.6, 7 and 8; furniture/price.py

Tamerlan (about 30 seconds). One recipe for every model: scaled sizes, one-hot colour and type, log price, the name as TF-IDF or embeddings, a photo as a frozen network’s vector. The 793 test listings are locked away and a design never crosses the split. Settings are chosen by grouped 5-fold CV inside one pipeline, and the winner runs on the test set once.
