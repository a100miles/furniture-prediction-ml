Method

The same recipe for every model

1

Preprocessing

Sizes scaled (StandardScaler); colour and type one-hot; 16 material flags. Price as log. Name as TF-IDF or e5 vectors; photo as a frozen network’s vector (384–512 numbers).

2

Split

793 test listings (92 designs) locked away since the Midterm. Colour variants of one design always stay together (groups). Seed 42.

3

Tuning

Stratified grouped 5-fold CV. GridSearchCV or RandomizedSearchCV inside one sklearn Pipeline, so nothing leaks from the folds. Pick by CV mean, then one test run.

4

Checks

Per-class precision, recall and PR-AUC; design names masked; six fold seeds; outside tests on Amazon, IKEA Saudi Arabia and IKEA photos.

Libraries: scikit-learn, LightGBM, PyTorch / torchvision, Hugging Face transformers, sentence-transformers. The chosen models are saved with joblib and served by the app (Starlette API + React).

final.ipynb, sections 0, 1.3, 1.6, 7 and 8; furniture/price.py

Tamerlan (about 40 seconds). Every model goes through the same recipe. Sizes are scaled, colour and type one-hot encoded, the price is predicted on a log scale, the name becomes TF-IDF or embedding vectors and a photo becomes the vector of a frozen network. The 793 test listings are locked away, and all colours of one design stay on the same side. Settings are searched with grouped 5-fold cross-validation inside one pipeline, the winner is picked by its CV mean and runs on the test set once. Then we check per-class scores, leakage through design names, other fold seeds and other stores.

Conclusions

What we can say, and what we cannot

Answered

Specifications price a listing within about 22–25%. The product name lowers the error by 3–5%, steadily, not by memorising designs, and also at IKEA Saudi Arabia (47% → 40%). Our own text features beat a pretrained encoder here. The type is a size question (0.918 from three sizes; the price adds nothing) and stays in its store (0.606 on Amazon). For photos, a better pretrained encoder (DINOv2, 0.851) beat training our own.

Limits

One store, one city, one day of prices. Text instead of photos for price, because the photos are not ours. CV numbers are slightly optimistic (tuned on the same folds); the test set has only 92 designs. The photo result is on other products.
